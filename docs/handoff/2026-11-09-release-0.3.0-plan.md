## What 0.3.0 contains

Full text: `CHANGELOG.md`, section `[Unreleased]`. In short:

**Method changes (results differ from 0.2.0)**
1. The year boundary reaches the end of the dry season on channels that dry out: the search starts from the end of the typical low (DE2, R1). Fitzroy tiles: years ending 3+ months before the dry season 251 → 104. Whole catchments essentially unchanged.
2. Every year reports when its dry season ends (dry-run start, end, re-wetting month, length, end status); the report timeline marks it (DE1, change A).
3. A boundary at the end of a dry, zero-extent low is labelled from the run end to the month before the next peak, never one exact month (DE2, revised R2).
4. A trough label always covers the trough boundary (0.3.0 change B).
5. Any dated year can anchor the condition baseline; about ten times as many areas get condition labels (0.3.0 change C, tested in B2).
6. The fingerprint covers every rule that shapes a published label.

**Additions without method change:** hydro-years CSV columns explaining provisional years and baseline use; two input warnings; shaded events and spells on aseasonal report timelines; `baseline_n` filled.

**Docs:** dates around the dry season, how far to trust a single trough month, choosing the area to analyse, all status and reason codes.

**Fixes (from the never-released 0.2.1):** inserted months keep outside-AOI cells; build needs `setuptools>=77`.

## Known limits to state in the release (not blockers)

- On ephemeral channels accuracy stays modest: on known truth about one dry-season end in four to three in ten is dated within a month among records with yearly results; a noisy dry month can end a dry run early.
- Most ephemeral records are routed to event characterisation and get no yearly results.
- In a year with no wet season the boundary is a placeholder at the typical month (for example Fitzroy tile 13, 2018 and 2019), flagged `unresolved`; the dry-run columns give the real end. A "no wet season" flag column was offered, not built.
- On ephemeral tiles more years have unresolved timing (408 → 533 of 1,600 tile-years), so they have no phases; about 5% fewer real-record areas have a condition baseline.
- The revised R2 label was checked after DE2 on the same records, not in a new pre-registered test.
- In whole catchments the dry-season end can trail the boundary by one or two months on a slow rise (Daly 4, Fitzroy 7, Gilbert 4 of 21 years at 30 m).
