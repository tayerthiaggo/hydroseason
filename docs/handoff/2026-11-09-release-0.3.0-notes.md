# HydroSeason 0.3.0

On channels that dry out, the hydrological year now ends where the dry season
ends, not at the first month of a flat low. Every year also reports when its
dry season ends (start, end and length of the dry run, and the re-wetting
month), and the report timeline marks it. Any dated year can now anchor the
condition baseline, so about ten times as many areas get recharge and refuge
labels. Whole catchments and most subbasins keep the same year boundaries.

## Method change: results differ from 0.2.0

0.3.0 runs method policy `hydroseason-v0.3.0` (fingerprint
`59756dfe895ffbe5213b558229eb36f1a0452e86e7c1d95e334bab31fd6bfb65`), which
replaces `hydroseason-v0.2.0`.

- On records with a flat low, boundaries move to the end of the dry season, and
  with them year starts and ends, some peaks, phases and baselines. On the
  Fitzroy grid tiles, years ending three or more months before the dry season
  fall from 251 to 104.
- A boundary at the end of a dry, zero-extent low is dated as a span from the
  run end to the month before the next peak, never a single month.
- A trough timing label always covers the trough boundary, so some `point`
  troughs become `interval`, `broad` or `unresolved`.
- `point`, `interval` and `broad` years can all anchor the condition baseline.
- The method fingerprint now covers every rule that shapes a published label.

## Migrating from 0.2.x

Compare 0.2.x and 0.3.0 output files only on columns that did not change. Tell
them apart by `method_policy_id` or the fingerprint. Columns that can differ:
trough timing labels, `baseline_eligible`, `baseline_n`, percentiles, condition
labels, monthly `phase` and `phase_status`; on records with a flat low, also
boundaries, year starts and ends, and some peaks. Full list:
[Changes from hydroseason-v0.2.0](https://tayerthiaggo.github.io/hydroseason/methods/#changes-from-hydroseason-v020).

## Also new

- Hydro-years CSV columns: why a year is partial or provisional, and whether
  it anchors the baseline (`status_reason`, `trough_refinement_status`,
  `trough_refinement_reason`, `baseline_eligible`, `baseline_n`), plus the
  dry-run columns. All appended after the existing columns.
- Two input warnings: an `extent_pct` that disagrees with the pixel counts, and
  a record without pixel counts whose detectability floor is effectively zero.
- Aseasonal report timelines shade wet events and low-water spells.
- Docs: the dates around the dry season, how far to trust a single trough
  month, choosing the area to analyse, and every status and reason code.

## Fixes

- Months inserted for missing scenes keep their outside-AOI cells.
- The build needs `setuptools>=77`.

## Known limits

- On ephemeral channels, accuracy stays modest: on known truth, about one
  dry-season end in four to three in ten is dated within a month, among records
  with yearly results. A noisy dry month can end a dry run early.
- Most ephemeral records are routed to event characterisation and get no
  yearly results.
- In a year with no wet season the boundary is a placeholder at the typical
  month, flagged `unresolved`; the dry-run columns give the real end.
- On ephemeral tiles more years have unresolved timing (408 to 533 of 1,600
  tile-years), so they have no phases; about 5% fewer real-record areas have a
  condition baseline.
- In whole catchments the dry-season end can trail the boundary by one or two
  months on a slow rise.
- The span label for a boundary at the end of a dry low was checked on the
  same records used to design it, not in a new pre-registered test.

HydroSeason measures surface-water extent, not flow. It is not referenced to
gauges.
