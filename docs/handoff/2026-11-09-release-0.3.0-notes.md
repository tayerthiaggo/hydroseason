# HydroSeason 0.3.0

On channels that dry out, the hydrological year now ends where the dry season
ends, not at the first month of a flat low. Every year also reports when its
dry season ends (start, end and length of the dry run, and the re-wetting
month), and the report timeline marks it. Any dated year can now anchor the
condition baseline, so about ten times as many areas get recharge and refuge
labels. Whole catchments and most subbasins keep the same year boundaries.

## Method change: results differ from 0.2.0

0.3.0 runs method policy `hydroseason-v0.3.0` (fingerprint
`07f1eaa173372e2544c46f794426917539a774756a64ccdac400ea0a747171cb`), which
replaces `hydroseason-v0.2.0`.

- On records with a flat low, boundaries move to the end of the dry season, and
  with them year starts and ends, some peaks, phases and baselines. On 40
  grid tiles over the Fitzroy catchment (WA), years ending three or more
  months before the dry season fall from 251 to 104, of about 1,600 tile-years.
- A boundary at the end of a dry, zero-extent low is dated as a span from the
  run end to the month before the next peak, never a single month.
- A trough timing label always covers the trough boundary, so some `point`
  troughs become `interval`, `broad` or `unresolved`.
- `point`, `interval` and `broad` years can all anchor the condition baseline.
- The method fingerprint now covers the timing-identifiability gates and the
  trough-label, condition-baseline and dry-season rules, and the trough-search,
  boundary-selection and peak-quality settings (values unchanged).

## Migrating from 0.2.x

Compare 0.2.x and 0.3.0 output files only on columns that did not change. Tell
them apart by `method_policy_id` or the fingerprint. Columns that can differ:
trough timing labels, `baseline_eligible`, `baseline_n`, percentiles, condition
labels, monthly `phase` and `phase_status`; on records with a flat low, also
boundaries, year starts and ends, and some peaks. Full list:
[Changes from hydroseason-v0.2.0](https://tayerthiaggo.github.io/hydroseason/methods/#changes-from-hydroseason-v020).

## Also new

- Hydro-years CSV columns: why a year is partial or provisional, and whether
  it anchors the baseline (`status_reason`, `trough_refinement_status`,
  `trough_refinement_reason`, `baseline_eligible`, `baseline_n`), plus the
  dry-run columns. All appended after the existing columns.
- Two input warnings: an `extent_pct` that disagrees with the pixel counts, and
  a record without pixel counts whose detectability floor is effectively zero.
- Aseasonal report timelines shade wet events and low-extent spells.
- Docs: the dates around the dry season, how far to trust a single trough
  month, choosing the area to analyse, and every status and reason code.
- Monthly `phase_status` is `interval_boundary` in `broad` years as well as
  `interval` years.
- Reports and the CLI refuse to overwrite the run's input or another
  catchment's files, and a CSV that cannot be written stops the run.
- The run manifest records the catchment name and the analysis settings used.
- Download caches re-fetch a year cached less than 60 days after it ended, so
  late-ingested scenes are picked up. The cache stores its write time, so a
  copied cache keeps it.
- Batch runs warn when several areas have no ID column, since they are then
  named by row position.
- Trough refinement is 2–3 times faster, with identical output.
- `measurement_tolerance_pct` defaults to 0.0 everywhere, as in
  `analyze_catchment`.

## Removed

- `phase_model` (deprecated in 0.2.0; use `phase_scheme`).
- `CatchmentAnalysis.public_route` (use `route`),
  `CatchmentReportPaths.events_csv` (use `wet_event_csv`), and three
  always-derivable `WaterRegimeAssessment` flags, without a deprecation
  period.
- `DynamicHydroYearConfig` parameters `dry_plateau_rule`,
  `sustained_rise_months` and `pulse_rejection_window_months` (ignored since
  0.1.0), the undocumented `hydroseason.io.probe_amplitude` and
  `hydroseason.io.mark_in_aoi_nodata_as_invalid`, and the undocumented
  `composite_bundle="legacy"` value. `composite_bundle` now
  rejects unknown values. The `case-study` extra is gone (use `stac`).

## Fixes

- The report's seasonality-evidence card no longer says both tests reject
  uniformity when recurrence was not established.
- A record without an `invalid_pct` column now gets hydrological years from
  `analyze_catchment`, and `value_col` works.
- Basin aggregation no longer counts missing areas as dry.
- NetCDF masks with `_FillValue=-2` are read correctly.
- `complete_monthly_axis(duplicate_month_policy="warn")` keeps the data.
- Months inserted for missing scenes keep their outside-AOI cells.
- Caches on Windows network shares (`\\server\share`) work; parallel year
  downloads work on Windows; two processes can no longer take one cache lock.
- `pip install hydroseason[raster]` pins `xarray<2026.9`, whose Zarr writer
  needs zarr 3.
- The build needs `setuptools>=77`.

## Known limits

- On ephemeral channels, accuracy stays modest: on records with known answers
  (synthetic seasons, and seasons injected into real records), about one
  dry-season end in four to three in ten is dated within a month, among records
  with yearly results. A noisy dry month can end a dry run early.
- Most ephemeral records are routed to event characterisation and get no
  yearly results.
- In a year with no wet season the boundary is a placeholder at the typical
  month, flagged `unresolved`; the dry-run columns give the real end.
- On the Fitzroy grid tiles more years have unresolved timing (408 to 533, of about 1,600
  tile-years), so they have no phases; about 5% fewer real-record areas have a
  condition baseline.
- In whole catchments the dry-season end can trail the boundary by one or two
  months on a slow rise.
- The span label for a boundary at the end of a dry low was checked on the
  same records used to design it, not in a new pre-registered test.
- When the typical trough falls early in the calendar year, a trough in the
  last months of the record lies in the search window of the year after the
  record, which is not searched, so the record's last year stays open. 0.2.0
  does the same. Searching that window adds a final year on about 13% of
  real records, mostly from an unconfirmed trough in the last month, so it
  waits for its own test.
- Cache locks may not hold on NFS shares; keep a shared cache on a local disk
  or a Windows share.

HydroSeason measures surface-water extent, not flow. It is not referenced to
gauges.
