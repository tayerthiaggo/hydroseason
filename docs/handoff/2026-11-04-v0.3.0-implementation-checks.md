## Step 3: five case studies

Script [`scripts/v030_case_study_comparison.py`](../../scripts/v030_case_study_comparison.py); output [2026-11-04-v0.3.0-case-study-comparison.json](2026-11-04-v0.3.0-case-study-comparison.json). Each of the five catchments was run at 30, 60, 90 and 300 m under both libraries and compared column by column, including the report tables.

- `unexpected_differences` is empty. Boundaries, dates, peaks, routes, monthly phases, events and low-extent spells are identical in all 20 series.
- B changes nothing here: every dated trough label already covered its boundary.
- C changes which cycles anchor the condition baseline:

| Catchment | 30 m | 60 m | 90 m | 300 m |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daly | 2 → 4 anchoring cycles, no labels | 2 → 5, labels in 21 years | 3 → 6, labels in 21 years | 2 → 4, no labels |
| Fitzroy | 1 → 7, labels in 21 years | 1 → 5, 21 years | 1 → 5, 21 years | 1 → 8, 20 years |
| Gilbert | 3 → 9, labels in 21 years | 3 → 9, 21 years | 4 → 8, 21 years | 4 → 6, 21 years |
| Lachlan, Moonie | event characterisation, unchanged | | | |

No series had condition labels under 0.2.0. The columns that change are the condition-baseline columns only: `baseline_eligible`, `baseline_n`, `peak_percentile`, `trough_percentile`, the recharge, refuge and annual conditions with their `_qualified` forms, and `consecutive_dry_cycles` / `consecutive_wet_cycles`.

**Selection effect.** The anchoring cycles are the years with a confirmed boundary, not a random sample of years. On Fitzroy at 30 m they are mostly the larger-flood years (median peak rank 0.76 against 0.40 for the other years), so 13 of 21 years read low recharge. On Gilbert at 30 m it is the reverse (0.33 against 0.69), and 9 of 21 read high recharge. The qualified annual label reads `typical_or_mixed` in 20 of 21 Fitzroy years and in all 21 Gilbert years. The same mechanism existed under 0.2.0 but almost no area had a baseline. B2's generators did not model it, and it has not been measured across the real-record units.

## Step 4: real-record rerun

Script [`scripts/v030_real_record_check.py`](../../scripts/v030_real_record_check.py); output [2026-11-04-v0.3.0-real-record-check.json](2026-11-04-v0.3.0-real-record-check.json). It reruns all 4,468 records of the [real-record impact measurement](2026-11-03-real-record-impact-results.md) (G1 windows and spatial-unit-study units) with the 0.3.0 library and compares them with the stored 0.2.0 runs, which are read and not rewritten.

**Pass criteria from the proposal:**
- All 4,468 records ran under `hydroseason-v0.3.0` with the fingerprint above; 0 errors.
- 0 route changes and 0 cycle-count changes.
- Only the columns B and C may touch differ; `unexpected_changed_fields` is empty.
- Dated trough labels that do not cover the boundary: 0 of 64,746. Before: 1,637 of 19,549 `point` troughs (8.4%) named a different month from the boundary. After: 0 of 17,912.
- The library's own labels (E0) reproduce the scorer's dated-baseline rule (E1) on every unit: `e0_mismatches_new` is 0.

**Change B against the simulation** ([label-fix impact](2026-11-04-v0.3.0-label-fix-impact.json)). For `point` troughs the rerun reproduces the simulation exactly:

| | G1 windows | Spatial-unit study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `point` troughs that change | 1,480 (simulated 1,480) | 157 (simulated 157) |
| … to `interval` / `broad` / `unresolved` | 627 / 486 / 367 (same) | 56 / 48 / 53 (same) |
| Cycles whose `timing_status` changes, from a `point` trough | 1,131 (simulated 1,131) | 138 (simulated 138) |

Widening only ever moves the interval's end month, never its start: every label that missed lay before its boundary.

**Gap the simulation did not model.** The rule applies to every dated trough label, not only `point` ones, and the simulation counted only `point` troughs. The rerun also widens 299 `interval` or `broad` labels (G1 267, spatial-unit study 32). 224 of them change grade, to `broad` or `unresolved`, and 75 keep it; they change `timing_status` on 198 more cycles. Its one consequence that matters is below.

**Coverage, units with a condition baseline** (per-year records only):

| Source | Units | 0.2.0 | C alone (stored runs) | 0.3.0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 core | 446 | 24 | 278 | 274 (61.4%) |
| G1 quadrant | 1,555 | 73 | 706 | 690 (44.4%) |
| Catchment | 3 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Subbasin level 6 / 7 / 8 | 23 / 101 / 292 | 1 / 3 / 5 | 14 / 51 / 92 | 14 / 51 / 92 |
| Tile 6 / 7 / 8 | 44 / 121 / 425 | 0 / 6 / 11 | 16 / 53 / 129 | 16 / 53 / 127 |
| **All** | **3,010** | **123 (4.1%)** | **1,340** | **1,318 (43.8%)** |

Refuge-labelled cycles rise from 4,232 to 44,052 of 97,227.

- 22 units have a baseline under C alone but not under 0.3.0, against 16 in the simulation. All sat at 5 or 6 anchoring cycles, the minimum being 5. Sixteen lose at least one through a `point` trough, matching the simulation's 16. The other six lose theirs only through `interval` or `broad` troughs that the widened label makes `unresolved`.
- Of the 123 units with a baseline under 0.2.0, 122 keep one. One (G1 `nat-018_155|q11|A`) had exactly 5 `point` cycles; one became `unresolved` and it has no other dated cycle, so its 38 cycles lose their labels.
- 138 cycles whose label became `unresolved` keep `status=complete` and `boundary_status=confirmed`, because B does not change cycle status. They cannot anchor the baseline.

**Labels on cycles labelled under both versions.** Refuge labels change on 607 of 3,648 G1 cycles (16.6%) and 94 of 546 spatial-unit cycles (17.2%), because the baseline they are ranked against grows. B2 found 10.3% (synthetic) and 15.0% (injected) on known truth.

## Step 5: known-truth rescore

Script [`scripts/v030_rescore_known_truth.py`](../../scripts/v030_rescore_known_truth.py); output [2026-11-04-v0.3.0-known-truth-rescore.json](2026-11-04-v0.3.0-known-truth-rescore.json). The stored L1 and L2 runs, rescored with rule B. Share of `point` troughs whose month is more than one month from the truth:

| | Before B | After B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 synthetic | 28.2% (784 of 2,779) | 28.3% (773 of 2,734) |
| L1 injected | 24.6% (1,192 of 4,837) | 23.3% (965 of 4,136) |
| L2 synthetic | 30.2% (899 of 2,981) | 30.1% (878 of 2,917) |
| L2 injected | 24.3% (1,148 of 4,724) | 24.3% (997 of 4,107) |

B makes the label agree with the reported trough month. It does not make `point` troughs closer to the true trough; the miss share stays at 23–30%, as L1 and L2 found.

## Deviations

- **Simulation scope.** The proposal's simulation counted only `point` troughs. B also widens `interval` and `broad` labels, which costs 6 more units their baseline than simulated (22 against 16). This was found after the run; the rule was not changed.
- **Run time.** The real-record rerun took 3 h 3 min (10,968 s) on 12 workers while other jobs ran on the machine.
- **Steps 1 and 8.** As recorded in the proposal: the branch starts from `spatial-unit-study`, not from `main`, and the package version is not yet 0.3.0.
