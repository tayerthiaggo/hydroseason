Does this catchment have a usable hydrological year?

Run before hydrological-year detection on any Australian catchment. Classifies the observed extent_pct record into one of three regimes and states what may — and may not — be computed from it, before a single peak or trough is reported.

Scope. Every verdict below describes observed surface water — water availability as seen from above — not rainfall and not climate. Regulation, extraction, diversion, and land-use change move extent independently of rainfall, so a flat or shifted signal is evidence about the catchment as observed, never proof of its natural condition.
Regime = hydroseason.assess_water_regime(). Seasonal ≥ 2.0 amplitude SNR with per-year peak-phase IQR ≤ 1.5 months; aseasonal < 0.7 SNR or ≥ 3.5 months phase spread; everything between is marginal. Verdicts shown are computed at 30 m and confirmed stable at 60 m (and at 90/300 m where extracted). Peak-month histograms count each catchment year's own argmax(extent_pct) — years with fewer than 9 usable months are excluded, not zero-filled.
